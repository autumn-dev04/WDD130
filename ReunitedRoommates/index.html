Welcome to Reunited Roommates!

This is a murder mystery ARG website where you are looking around to find out who died, who killed them, and why? Provided for you to explore is an obituary page and a blog page. This is my first time making an ARG website like this so it probably won't be perfect but I'm doing my best and I hope you enjoy it! Obviously none of the events in this story are actually true. Everything you are about to discover is completely fictional and staged. Special thanks to all my amazing friends who have been willing to help me create this strange project and bring this story to life!
