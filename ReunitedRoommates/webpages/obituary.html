In loving Memory of Jessica Mae Darwin

Jessica Mae Darwin, 31, of New York was found dead in the Center Hotel off of 87th street on Monday, September 7th, 20XX. She was born on December 18th, 20XX at 4:17pm to Lily Gomez and Jackson Darwin in the big city of New York, New York. Jessica Darwin had been attending a class reunion at Oak Hills University. She graduated 10 years prior with a bachelor's degree in nursing. Jessica worked diligently to help those around her and progress in her career at the prestigious St. Frank's Hospital. She spent most of her days working, but when she wasn't at the hospital helping new mothers and babies, she enjoyed cooking, baking, and playing with her hamster named Pepperment. Jessica is survived by her parents, Lily and Jackson, and her loving fiance, Samson White. The funeral service will be held at the Church of the Rood on 23rd street at 10am on Saturday, September 12th, 20XX. In lieu of flowers, Lily Gomez asks for prayers for Samson White as he grieves the loss of his beloved fiancee.

"Jessica was my pride and joy. She was the sun that rose every morning to scare away my darkness. Now we are alone and must fight this darkness without her. We will miss her and we hope to rest easy knowing that her soul now dwells with those who came before her." - Lily Gomez
